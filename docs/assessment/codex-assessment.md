## Recommendation

Publish a small **DV-2026 / v2026.0** edition from independently collected, permission-respecting **2026 in-store thrift shelf-price observations**, explicitly described as a late-2026, multi-location convenience sample. Target **50 independently specified common items**, publishing only cells meeting the predeclared evidence rules. Do not promise national representativeness, condition-specific price tiers, or a complete DeductIt replacement catalog. Aim for **January 15, 2027** publication, subject to human decisions and sampling. The December 29 target in my first assessment leaves too little time for post-comment approval and final evidence review. A January 2027 publication using documented 2026 observations still can be a tax-year-2026 edition; 2027 observations cannot silently become 2026 evidence.

Use no ItsDeductible values, labels, hierarchy, synonyms, mappings, ordering or model-derived imitations. Use no marketplace data for either published estimates or internal calibration without explicit written rights. The release should be a small, auditable reference, not a renamed proprietary guide or an AI-generated price table.

## Minimum viable methodology

Publish `METHODOLOGY-2026.0` as a new, substantively revised draft. Retain the 2027 draft history. Public review must cover the reduced scope and revised estimator, not just a year substitution.

1. **Scope:** independently draft about 50 measurable specs from observed common household inventory and IRS broad groups, with unit, material/size where relevant and objective inclusion checklist. Exclude luxury/vintage/collectibles, jewelry, vehicles, broken/untested electronics and safety-sensitive goods. No Google taxonomy import is necessary for MVP; add a separately rights-reviewed crosswalk later. Freeze specs before collecting; report omissions, not substitutes.
2. **Collection:** target 18 stores in six metros spanning at least three Census divisions and multiple operators. Design assignments so each published spec has at least **30 unique item observations, six stores, three metros and three independent pricing authorities**; cap each store's effective weight at 20% and any metro at 50% of a cell; require at least three independently controlled pricing authorities and cap each at 40% of both accepted observations and estimator weight. Withhold any cell failing the count or weight caps; do not trim valid records or tune weights to rescue it. At most five physical items per spec/store visit; prevent repeat counts across visits using evidence/item identities. Identical prices on distinct items are not duplicates.
3. **Estimator:** equal-store-weighted empirical price distribution, p25/median/p75 labelled lower/typical/upper **sample prices**, not basic/good/premium condition valuations. Record full tag cents and generally available markdown amount/rule/effective cents separately; use current unconditional payable offer price, excluding tax, as headline. No loyalty-only discounts or assumed coupons. Publish tag-only sensitivity. No platform-fee subtraction: seller net proceeds are not buyer-paid FMV.
4. **Quality:** saleable stock is not automatically good condition. Inspect condition/working/completeness explicitly. Record refusals, missing stock, units, dates, collector certification, permission and photo/attestation evidence. No closest-match substitution into another spec. Review outliers without silently deleting inconvenient prices. Separate private evidence and public records; never export private evidence URIs/identities.
5. **Sampling honesty:** this is a purposive convenience sample, not a national probability estimate. Geographic minima improve diversity without proving representativeness. Use a seeded **cluster bootstrap accounting for pricing-authority/store dependence**, reviewed by a statistician, and describe its uncertainty as conditional on sampled stores. Do not let thousands of tags at one chain masquerade as independent national evidence. No regional estimates, premium cells, missing-cell inheritance or CPI-generated values in MVP.
6. **Coverage:** 50 × 30 = **1,500 accepted observations minimum**; budget **2,000–3,000 raw** for uneven stock/QA. At 40–60 usable records/hour, about 40–75 collection hours plus travel, permission and review; 18 visits of 3–5 hours is a reasonable scheduling envelope, not a guarantee every cell fills. Reduce to 25–40 qualified specs if needed, keeping thresholds fixed. Publish no value for insufficient evidence; do not fill the gaps from ItsDeductible or AI.
7. **Time:** collect November/December 2026 and publish the actual window on every item. These observations do not prove January 2026 prices. Do not claim values were available at the donation date when published later. Late-year estimates may be reference evidence; donor-specific contemporaneous evidence can be better.

Schema changes are necessary: effective/tag price distinction, evidence and permissions manifest, sample scope, new quantile labels, quality coverage and per-cell contributor/store restrictions. Structural validation alone is inadequate. No LLM pricing, brand-tier classifier, submission web app, Parquet or SQLite is required for the first release. CSV + JSON + static pages, deterministic standard aggregation libraries and integer cents are enough; explicitly revise the existing broader promises.
