# Code of Conduct

DonationValues follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep disagreements about the
methodology on the evidence, and never post personal information about
collectors, store staff, shoppers or donors.

Reports go to conduct@donationvalues.org. Maintainers will acknowledge within
five business days and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently remove
anyone who violates this code.
