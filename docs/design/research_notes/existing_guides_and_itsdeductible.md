# Existing US donation valuation guides, tools and datasets — and the ItsDeductible timeline

Research snapshot dated 2026-11-02. All values are USD unless noted. "Fetched" means the page was retrieved and read on that date; "search snippet" means the fact comes from a search-engine summary of a page that could not be fetched (page 404/403, or Wayback blocked in this environment) and should be re-verified before being quoted publicly.

## Key Question 1 — ItsDeductible (Income Dynamics / Intuit): history, methodology, condition tiers, discontinuation, export terms

### Takeaway
ItsDeductible began around 2000 as a $29.95 Omaha product (Income Dynamics) whose values came from 13 years of annual surveys of thrift and consignment shops, later supplemented with eBay closed-auction feeds; Intuit bought the company in July 2003 for ~$10M, tried to shut the tool on Oct 17, 2022 (reversed after user backlash), and did shut it on Oct 21, 2025. Users got CSV/PDF/ZIP exports of their own entries before shutdown and, after Nov 25, 2025, an Intuit-account "Data privacy > Download" dump (ItsDeductible data in a `SharedData` subfolder); no evidence was found that Intuit ever offered the valuation database itself for download, and no download-specific license exists beyond Intuit's site-wide Terms of Use.

### Cited Findings

**Origins and pre-Intuit methodology (2000–2003)**
- April 4, 2002 profile: ItsDeductible was made by Income Dynamics, based in Omaha; the company had sold it "as a software program" for "the past two years" (i.e., since roughly 2000) at $29.95. For 13 years the company "surveyed thrift and consignment shops across the country" to find going rates for donated items and had published the findings annually in book form before offering software. Example value given: a long-sleeve men's dress shirt in good condition, $16. The article names only Bill Reed, VP of marketing and distribution; no founder is named. — [Chronicle of Philanthropy, "Software Aids Taxpayers Who Donate Goods" (fetched)](https://www.philanthropy.com/news/software-aids-taxpayers-who-donate-goods/)
- Dec 5, 2001: H&R Block and Income Dynamics announced a marketing alliance letting TaxCut users buy ItsDeductible at a reduced $19.95 for the 2001 tax season; product described as determining and tracking the proper "fair market value" of item donations. — [H&R Block investor release (search snippet)](https://investors.hrblock.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hr-block-inc-and-income-dynamics-inc-announce-marketing-alliance)
- Intuit's FY2003 10-K: "In July 2003 we acquired all of the outstanding stock of Income Dynamics, Inc. for approximately $10.0 million or 224,589 shares of Intuit common stock." The software "provides tools for taxpayers to determine the fair market value of items donated to charities"; Income Dynamics became part of the Consumer Tax segment; ~$3.2M allocated to identified intangibles, $7.9M goodwill. — [Intuit 10-K FY2003 (search snippet)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000896878/000089161803004854/f90545e10vk.htm)
- Trade press: all-stock deal worth ~$10M closed July 11, 2003; Intuit had an exclusive distribution arrangement with Income Dynamics the prior year (revenue share), and Income Dynamics had had a similar exclusive with H&R Block the year before that; Intuit cited ~100,000 existing ItsDeductible customers. — [InternetNews (search snippet)](https://internetnews.com/small-business/intuit-acquisition-bulks-up-e-tax-offerings); [Accounting Today, July 18, 2003 (search snippet)](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/intuit-acquires-income-dynamics)

**Methodology Intuit/Income Dynamics publicly claimed**
- Feb 12, 2006 blog quoting the product FAQ: values use "patent-pending Intelligent Indexing™", are "based on extensive research of resale outlets as well as recent market data from eBay®", using "daily feeds of closed live auction transaction pricing information"; the user enters an item and the tool searches an archive of eBay auction data for an average market value. The author found items they had valued at $1 came back at "$4, $6 or even $10". — [tins.rklau.com, "I want my money back" (fetched)](https://tins.rklau.com/2006/02/i-want-my-money-back/)
- ItsDeductible's own help pages (still indexed, now 404 when fetched 2026-11-02): "ItsDeductible has three value categories to help you assess the fair market value of your donated items: High, Medium and Low." The software "collects and analyzes pricing information from multiple data sources including various thrift and consignment stores." It "does not appraise your specific item"; "It is your responsibility to determine if your item is comparable to the examples in ItsDeductible, or if there is a better indicator of its value for donation purposes." — [itsdeductibleonline.intuit.com/help/additemhuid.html (search snippet; 404 on fetch)](https://itsdeductibleonline.intuit.com/help/additemhuid.html); [itsdeductibleonline.intuit.com/help/learnmoreitem.html (search snippet; 404 on fetch)](https://itsdeductibleonline.intuit.com/help/learnmoreitem.html)
- Pension Protection Act handling: "ItsDeductible will not allow you to make any entries for clothing and household items in the Low value column if your donation is after August 17 [2006]" because the Act bars deductions for clothing/household items not in good used condition or better; Low entries remained allowed for earlier donations. — [itsdeductibleonline.intuit.com/help/irslaw_aug172006.html (search snippet)](https://itsdeductibleonline.intuit.com/help/irslaw_aug172006.html)
- A June 29, 2007 blog's comments describe the tool as having "a three-tier categorization" and using "recent eBay sales data to determine fair market value for each item." — [MyMoneyBlog (fetched)](https://mymoneyblog.com/new-rules-on-tax-deductible-donations-of-clothing-and-household-items.html)
- TurboTax's Form 8283 interview asks for the "method used to determine value"; community volunteers steer users to "Thrift shop value" and note "Fair Market Value" is not one of the listed options (Jan 29–31, 2025 thread). — [TurboTax Community (fetched)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/taxes/discussion/re-charitable-deduction-method-used-to-determine-value/01/3446126/highlight/true)

**2022 attempted shutdown and reversal**
- Email subject "Notice of Discontinuation TurboTax ItsDeductible Donation Valuation Tool": "We have made a decision to discontinue TurboTax ItsDeductible." "We are providing you with advance notice that ItsDeductible will be discontinued on October 17, 2022." "you will need to export any data you have entered into ItsDeductible that you want to save". "After that date you will no longer have access to ItsDeductible and any data you entered into it will be deleted." Reversal email "Exactly one month and one day later", subject "Update: ItsDeductible Continued Availability": "We heard your feedback on ItsDeductible and we listened!" "we are happy to share that we have revised our decision and will continue to support ItsDeductible next year." "Your account and the data in it will continue to be available without interruption." — [Dan Gingiss (fetched)](https://dangingiss.com/?p=5843)
- The reversal email was posted to a Motley Fool board on July 5, 2022 ("ItsBack!" thread, user FuskieBP), which places the original notice around early June 2022. — [Motley Fool discussion (fetched)](https://discussion.fool.com/t/itsback/72172); contradicted by [Deductible Duck (fetched)](https://deductibleduck.com/what-happened-to-itsdeductible/), which says the 2022 notice went out "In September 2022" — the dated Fool post is the stronger source.

**2025 shutdown**
- Users reported notices stating "On October 21, 2025, ItsDeductible will be discontinued" and were told to export data for 2021–2025 before that date. — [search summary of TurboTax Community/Intuit support (no primary Intuit notice URL located)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/get-your-taxes-done-using-turbotax-6/request-to-reconsider-discontinuation-of-itsdeductible-117547)

- Sep 5, 2025: original poster references a "recent notice" of October discontinuation; another user says Intuit's Office of the President called the decision "firm". Oct 21, 2025: volunteer M-MTax: "It's already gone." Oct 22, 2025: a user says the app was shut before the expected export deadline ("believed they had until midnight tonight to export their data"). No Intuit employee posts and no stated reason appear in the thread. — [TurboTax Community (fetched)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/get-your-taxes-done-using-turbotax-6/request-to-reconsider-discontinuation-of-itsdeductible-117547)
- Nov 18, 2025, M-MTax: "The last day was 10/20/25 which was extended for several days, but it appears to be gone at this point." (Note this reads as the service lasting a few days past Oct 20, which conflicts with the "shut a day early" reports above.) — [TurboTax Community (fetched)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/deductions-credits-9/itsdeductible-data-export-801792)
- The iOS app (id 725029107) was removed from the US App Store and is no longer live. — [AppGoblin (search snippet)](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/725029107)
- Intuit gave no detailed public reason; Deductible Duck's reasons (fewer itemizers, maintenance cost, focus on paid filing) are explicitly its own speculation. — [Deductible Duck (fetched)](https://deductibleduck.com/what-happened-to-itsdeductible/)

**What Intuit offered users on shutdown, and the terms**
- Pre-shutdown export: ZIP archives or separate Item, Money, Mileage and Stock CSV files (per Deductible Duck, which imports them). — [Deductible Duck (fetched)](https://deductibleduck.com/what-happened-to-itsdeductible/) ; community threads describe sign in → pick year → "Download my data" → CSV or PDF (search snippet, [TurboTax Community](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/taxes/discussion/how-can-i-import-my-charitable-deductions-into-turbotax-2025-that-i-exported-out-of-itsdeductible/00/3722738))
- Post-shutdown: Nov 25, 2025, Intuit moderator GabiU: "It's now possible to download your ItsDeductible data," via the article "How to access and manage your personal data from your Intuit Account." — [TurboTax Community (fetched)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/deductions-credits-9/itsdeductible-data-export-801792)
- That article (updated Feb 4, 2026) has a heading "I want to download my ItsDeductible data" and says: "When you receive your personal data file, your ItsDeductible data will be inside the SharedData subfolder." "Requests can take up to 15 days, depending on how much data is involved." "You'll get an email once your data is ready, with instructions for downloading." The article carries no download-specific terms; the only legal text is site-wide: "By accessing and using this page you agree to the Terms of Use" and "Terms and conditions, features, support, pricing, and service options subject to change without notice." — [Intuit help article (fetched)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/tax-topics/help/manage-your-intuit-data/00/971878)
- The Intuit redirect `support.turbotax.intuit.com/redirect/export-ItsDeductible-data` now resolves (via redirectlambda.api.intuit.com) to a generic "How do I enter my charitable donations in TurboTax?" article (updated April 15, 2026) that does not mention ItsDeductible at all. — [Intuit help article (fetched)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/turbotax-support/en-us/help-article/tax-credits-deductions/enter-2021-charitable-donations-turbotax/L4hj07Piz_US_en_US)
- The account-download dump is described by a competitor as an activity file covering the whole account history, "not consistent with past ItsDeductible exports" and not laid out for import. — [Deductible Duck (fetched)](https://deductibleduck.com/what-happened-to-itsdeductible/)
- A Jan 2026 community user reported Intuit customer service saying user data was permanently deleted; this conflicts with the Nov 2025 moderator statement that downloads work. — [search summary of TurboTax Community](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/taxes/discussion/its-deductible/00/3775627)
- TurboTax after ItsDeductible: TurboTax Online "does not currently provide a supported way for users to import itemized donation data from an imported file"; an Intuit moderator said Intuit is working on a data-file import "hopefully ... for the 2026 tax year at the latest." TurboTax Desktop still accepts TXF files. — [TurboTax Community (search snippet)](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/deductions-credits-9/itsdeductible-alternatives-801772); [Deductible Duck (fetched)](https://deductibleduck.com/what-happened-to-itsdeductible/)

### Inferences
- The lineage of ItsDeductible's numbers is "thrift/consignment shop survey (Income Dynamics, 1987–2003) + eBay closed-auction feed (added by ~2006)"; Intuit never published an updated methodology statement after the mid-2000s help pages, so the 2025-era values were most likely a maintained table rather than live market data.
- Intuit's 2001–2003 pattern (exclusive with H&R Block, then exclusive with Intuit, then acquisition) suggests the value database was always proprietary; nothing found indicates it was ever licensed to third parties or released on shutdown.
- The lack of a download-specific license means exported entries are the user's own records; the valuation figures inside those exports are Intuit's, and no explicit grant to reuse them exists.

### Gaps
- No primary Intuit URL for the 2025 discontinuation notice was located (it was emailed; the support redirect now points elsewhere; Wayback is blocked in this environment).
- No evidence Intuit offered a downloadable copy of the valuation list/database; only users' own entries were exportable.
- Exact 2022 notice date (early June vs. September) is unresolved between sources.
- The precise launch date of the first ItsDeductible software (c. 2000) and the earlier book title are unconfirmed; the Omaha company's founders are unnamed in sources found.
- Could not fetch the live 2024–2025 ItsDeductible marketing page (Wayback blocked), so any "audit-defensible" marketing phrasing is unverified.

## Key Question 2 — Salvation Army Donation Value Guide

### Takeaway
The Salvation Army guide (satruck.org/donation-value-guide, © 2026) is a low/high range table with no methodology, no last-updated date and no terms of use; it explicitly disclaims setting any valuation.

### Cited Findings
- URL: https://satruck.org/donation-value-guide. Page text: it "helps you determine the approximate tax-deductible value of some of the more commonly donated items." "Please choose a value within this range that reflects your item's relative age and quality." "The Salvation Army does not set a valuation on your donation. It's up to you to assign a value to your item." "For professional advice, please consult your tax advisor." "This list is not all-inclusive, and not all items are accepted at every location." Footer "Copyright © 2026 The Salvation Army"; no last-updated date; no terms-of-use text, only a Privacy Policy link. — [satruck.org (fetched)](https://satruck.org/donation-value-guide)
- Sample values (low–high): Men's shirt $3–$12; Men's jacket $8–$26; Men's overcoat $16–$62; Men's shoes $4–$26; Women's blouse $3–$12; Women's coat $10–$41; Women's shoes $2–$26; Children's jeans $4–$12 (no adult jeans line); Sofa $36–$207; Lamp $5–$78; Book hardback $1–$3; paperback $1–$2. — [satruck.org (fetched)](https://satruck.org/donation-value-guide)
- Regional Salvation Army pages (e.g., Lehigh Valley, Central USA) host the same guide, described as for "items commonly donated to The Salvation Army Family Thrift Stores." — [SA Lehigh Valley (search snippet)](https://pa.salvationarmy.org/lehigh-valley/Determining-Value-of-Donation)

### Inferences
- Values appear to be thrift-store resale ranges, but the page never says so; nothing supports citing them as a survey of store prices.

### Gaps
- No last-updated date, revision history, or methodology disclosed; no reuse terms.

## Key Question 3 — Goodwill guides, national vs regional, and in-store pricing

### Takeaway
Goodwill Industries International (GII) does publish a national "Valuation Guide for Goodwill Donors" (two variants circulate: a single-value one-pager and a low/high range table), and regional members republish or adapt it (e.g., Goodwill Northern New England adds a "30% of original price" rule); in stores Goodwill uses colored-tag weekly discount rotation and by-the-pound outlets, with no public per-item price list.

### Cited Findings

**GII national guide**
- Header text: "The U.S. Internal Revenue Service (IRS) requires donors to value their items. To help guide you, Goodwill Industries International has compiled a list providing price ranges for items commonly sold in Goodwill® stores. Assume the following items are in good condition, and remember: prices are only estimated values." Footer: "www.goodwill.org | 1-800-GOODWILL | 15810 Indianola Drive, Rockville, MD 20855". Single-value variant (PDF created June 13, 2017, hosted by MTSU): Shirt/Blouse $4; Jeans $6; Coat/Jacket $8; Shoes $6; Business Suit $12; Dress $7; Pants/Skirts $5; Sweater $5; Lamp $6; Book $1; Standard Sofa $75; Dresser $25; Desktop Computer $150. — [MTSU-hosted GII guide PDF (fetched, text extracted)](https://w1.mtsu.edu/marketing/docs/Donation_Valuation_Guide.pdf)

- A Goodwill of Greater Maryland PDF (`goodwill_donated_value_guide-20231013.pdf`) was indexed as linking to amazinggoodwill.com donor guidelines and a "donation receipt builder", but returned 404 on fetch. — [Goodwill Greater Maryland (404 on fetch)](https://www.goodwillgreatermc.org/docs/default-source/donations/goodwill_donated_value_guide-20231013.pdf)

**Goodwill Northern New England (regional)**

**Other regionals**

"Buy The Pound" outlets charge by weight, with bins "being swapped every hour or less." 20% senior/veteran discount Tuesdays. No flat per-category prices or boutique pricing described. — [Goodwill NNE shop (fetched)](https://goodwillnne.org/shop/)

- Salvation Army Family Stores: no published price list; a local store manager quoted "Our average price is under $4". — [TBS News (search snippet)](https://www.tbsnews.net/thoughts/why-goodwill-now-and-future-king-thrift-484706); Lewisville TX store promo: Wednesdays all clothing/shoes half-price. — [Community Impact (search snippet)](https://communityimpact.com/lewisville-flower-mound-highland-village/the-salvation-army-to-open-its-largest-family-store-in-the-dfw-metroplex-in-lewisville/)
- Goodwill network revenue: exceeded $7 billion in 2025, a record and +7% YoY, across ~3,400 stores. — [NBC Palm Springs, Jan 27, 2026 (search snippet)](https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/2026/01/27/goodwill-reports-record-7-billion-in-revenue-as-shoppers-look-to-save); [WFTV (search snippet)](https://www.wftv.com/news/local/goodwill-reports-7b-revenue-amid-economic-uncertainty/ISCXYHXD2ZAUTNXNR5KX5E2YBQ)

### Inferences
- The GII guide is the de facto "national" Goodwill guide, but it is distributed as an undated PDF without license; regional members copy it verbatim or substitute their own store-price ranges (NNE's figures differ materially, e.g., sofa up to $395 vs GII $150).
- Goodwill NNE's "30% of original price" rule is the only algorithmic fallback any guide discloses.
- Goodwill's colored-tag rotation means a single item's shelf price can be 25–100% of tag price depending on week, which complicates using shelf prices as FMV without a stated reference point.

### Gaps
- No Goodwill (GII or regional) annual report found that discloses items sold or revenue per item.
- No official per-item price lists for Goodwill, Savers, St. Vincent de Paul or Salvation Army stores exist publicly; only anecdotal quotes.
- Could not confirm whether goodwillaz.org or amazinggoodwill.com currently host valuation guides.

## Key Question 4 — Other tools and apps (incumbent and post-ItsDeductible)

### Takeaway
H&R Block DeductionPro and TaxAct Donation Assistant still ship inside paid tax products with no public methodology; CharityDeductions.com is now an empty placeholder site; and at least six consumer replacements (DeductIt, DeductAble, Deductible Duck, Deductible.me, That's a Write-Off, ItsDeducted) appeared between Oct 2025 and Mar 2026, mostly citing "thrift store prices and eBay data" without publishing datasets.

### Cited Findings
- **H&R Block DeductionPro**: not discontinued. Support page says it is usable in Deluxe, Premium or Self-Employed online products (page returned 403 on direct fetch; content from search index). A 2025 Premium retail listing says "H&R Block DeductionPro values and optimizes charitable donations (Schedule A)". A free download was offered as early as Jan 7, 2002; Consumerist listed it as a free tool in Jan 2007; an investor document described a "new, more advanced DeductionPro, free with every TaxCut Premium purchase." No methodology or condition tiers found. — [H&R Block support (search snippet)](https://www.hrblock.com/tax-center/support/online/while-filing-online-return/using-deductionpro/); [Quill product listing (search snippet)](https://www.quill.com/hr-block-tax-software-deluxe-state-2025-1-user-win-mac-download-hrb641800v017/cbs/55507925.html); [AnandTech forum, Jan 7, 2002 (search snippet)](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/free-download-of-h-r-block-deduction-pro-tax-software.1787551); [Consumerist, Jan 11, 2007 (search snippet)](https://consumerist.com/2007/01/11/two-free-tax-tools-deductionpro-and-organizit)
- **TaxAct Donation Assistant**: "a useful way to calculate the Fair Market Value (FMV) of donated items"; "provides certified resale values" (no explanation of source); only in Deluxe, Premier, Self-Employed; page metadata updated 2026-10-06. Other TaxAct pages claim "resale values for over 1,300 items", valuations "in accordance with IRS guidelines" and an "audit protection guarantee at no additional charge." Origin: 2016 Blucora press release announcing a partnership with CharityDeductions.com for TaxAct. — [TaxAct support (fetched)](https://support.taxact.com/support/1051/donation-assistant-general-information); [TaxAct support 766 (search snippet)](https://www.taxact.com/support/766); [Benzinga/Blucora release (search snippet)](https://www.benzinga.com/node/8747129)
- **CharityDeductions.com**: homepage now shows only "Charity Deductions — Maximize Your Tax Benefits", a contact form, and "Copyright © 2026 Charity Deductions - All Rights Reserved" on a GoDaddy site builder; no product, pricing or methodology. A competitor says it is "recommended by CharityDeductions.com's previous owner." — [charitydeductions.com (fetched)](https://www.charitydeductions.com/); [Deductible Duck (search snippet)](https://deductibleduck.com/)

App Store first release Feb 11, 2026 per a third-party listing. — [deductit.io (fetched)](https://deductit.io/); [mwm.ai listing (search snippet)](https://mwm.ai/apps/deductit-donation-tracker/6754351724)

## Key Question 6 — Academic / industry research on secondhand clothing prices

### Takeaway
Peer-reviewed quantitative studies of US thrift pricing are essentially absent; what exists is small qualitative work, market-size reports, and one large non-US industry dataset.

### Cited Findings
- Qualitative study of 19 thrift-store personnel interviews (Canada, New Zealand): secondhand clothing economy is "a highly contextual, variable space, impacted by the local environment and the personnel involved." — [PLATE 2025 proceedings, AAU (search snippet)](https://journals.aau.dk/index.php/plate2025/article/download/10275/8192/33865)
- Undergraduate honors research (10 online resellers, 7 thrift employees): most attributed price increases to "economic conditions and corporate pricing strategies"; authors note small sample. — [JWU Scholars Archive (search snippet)](https://scholarsarchive.jwu.edu/student_scholarship/72); [ITAA proceedings (search snippet)](https://www.iastatedigitalpress.com/itaa/article/id/22029/)
- Industry-commissioned El Salvador study of 21.8 million secondhand garments: 99.56% sold under $15; $3 the most common price; structured markdowns over 8–12 weeks. Non-US. — [Just Style (search snippet)](https://www.just-style.com/?p=214095)
- Tax Court denied a $49K clothing-donation deduction (case not named in snippet). — [Accounting Today (search snippet)](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/tax-court-no-dice-on-49k-clothing-deduction)
- NARTS / Association of Resale Professionals: no data surfaced in this pass.

### Inferences
- Citable price evidence for a methodology page will have to come from the guides themselves plus original sampling, not from literature.

### Gaps
- No NARTS pricing data, no Goodwill revenue-per-item disclosure, no US academic price study located.
